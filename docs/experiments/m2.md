## Notes

- Test-split probabilities are saved with each run for the ensemble but not scored; the test split is scored once, after the 11/18 experiment freeze.
- kNN on FastText vectors is well below the TF-IDF baseline (macro-F1 0.582 on the same split). With about 1,000 training tweets per fold, FastText's nearest neighbours are driven by spelling: *dip* is closest to *sip* and *sit*, *bear* to *beat* and *swear*. The largest error cells in the out-of-fold confusion matrix are Negative predicted as Positive (167) and Positive predicted as Negative (143).
- Confident learning flags 38.6% of each fold's training part. VADER disagreements are flagged at 73% against 69% for the other tweets, so the two checks barely agree with a weak embedding.
